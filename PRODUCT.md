# TEMO Energy

## Register

product

## Users

European electricity market analysts, renewable asset operators, and B2B customers evaluating an analytics portal. They compare bidding zones and inspect prices, generation, and interconnections at a desk throughout the trading day.

## Product Purpose

A customer portal for published electricity, gas and weather observations. It provides authenticated access, source attribution, native history, accurate derived statistics and visible coverage gaps. The hosted portal reads the full local market database through an authenticated outbound agent.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, informed. Bloomberg and TradingView inspire the information density; the interface should be readable and navigable by a first-time customer.

## Anti-references

Generic admin templates, oversized decorative heroes, rainbow dashboards, empty demonstration screens, and unnecessary animation.

## Design Principles

Make reported market conditions understandable at a glance. Show only implemented metrics backed by provider observations. Keep higher-tier capabilities discoverable. Display interval and collection times, preserve gaps and analytical context, and make each control perform a useful action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation baseline: keyboard navigation, explicit form labels, visible focus, sufficient contrast, text alongside semantic colors, reduced-motion support, and responsive tables and navigation.
