# TEMO visual system

## Scene and strategy

An analyst monitors European markets on a desktop display in a subdued trading office. The explicitly requested dark charcoal and navy surfaces reduce large bright regions; mint draws attention only to selected actions, renewable data, and feed health. Restrained color strategy. The supplied brief takes precedence over the palette script's violet seed; muted violet is reserved for a comparison series.

## Color

Background oklch(0.165 0.012 250), sidebar oklch(0.145 0.012 250), panels oklch(0.195 0.012 250), raised controls oklch(0.235 0.012 250). Main ink oklch(0.95 0.004 250), secondary ink oklch(0.72 0.014 250), borders oklch(0.29 0.012 250). Primary mint oklch(0.86 0.12 163), cyan, muted violet and amber distinguish chart series.

## Typography

Inter Variable, locally bundled. Fixed rem scale with 16px body text, 14px controls and table text, and a 12px minimum for captions, badges, and chart labels. Panel headings are at least 16px; page headings remain approximately 30px. Narrower screens reflow content instead of shrinking text. Secondary text has increased contrast. Tabular numbers and moderate 500–600 weights.

## Layout and components

256px persistent desktop sidebar, 72px top bar, 28px content inset. Navigation becomes a drawer at 1050px so readable controls retain sufficient space. Panels have a single subtle border, 10px radius, no decorative shadow. Main chart receives approximately two-thirds of its row. KPI values align consistently; supporting statistics form a compact rail. Standard native selects, labelled form inputs, semantic tables, and native dialogs. Tables scroll within their panels when needed.

## Motion

150–200ms state transitions; a brief highlight on updated values. No entrance choreography. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
